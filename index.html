A Call to Awaken

Rise Up, This Generation!

A blessed day, brothers and sisters in Christ!

We are excited and looking forward to having you join us as we come together to praise and worship our great and mighty God!

AWAKEN 2027 is a call for this generation to rise up, renew their faith, and shine the light of Christ. It is a reminder that we are not called to remain asleep but to live with purpose, faith, and a heart devoted to God.

Let us gather together in unity, worship, and fellowship as we glorify our one true and living God.
